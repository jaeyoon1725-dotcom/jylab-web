Healthcare — Self PT · AI Health Coach

Log workouts in the free Self PT app; the KakaoTalk AI coach reads your watch, InBody and meal photos together. For lifestyle coaching, not diagnosis or treatment.

Problem

Plenty of records, no conclusion

Data is scattered across watch, body-composition and meal apps — deciding what to actually do today stays hard.

Product

Free workout app + chat coaching

Goal-based routines and set logging are free; connect your data and it becomes a one-line coaching message every morning.

Private beta — 30 members, 4 weeks

We are recruiting beta members and reviewing data connection, morning coaching and weekly reports together.

Division · Hardware

Hardware — AquaPad

A smart coaster that logs your water intake automatically. Keep using your own cup — just set it on the coaster and your daily intake shows up in the app.

Problem

Water-tracking never lasts

Hydration apps rely on manual input, so the habit breaks within days. When logging stops, so does the care.

Approach

Zero behavior change

No special bottle, no buttons, no typing. Use the cup you already have; the device does the logging.

Now

First prototype in progress

We brought in 3D-printing equipment and are building the first prototype. The build process will be shared as dev notes.

Prototype stage · launch timeline via dev notes
Division · Education

Education — Tori, the AI Tutor

Elementary AI education that asks at the child's pace and trains the habit of verifying what AI says.

Problem

In the AI era, verification is the skill

AI gives the answers. What children need is the ability to check whether those answers are right.

Approach

Conversational learning + verification training

Dialog-based learning adjusted to grade and level, focused on checking and questioning rather than answers.

Now

Authoring the first course

The master curriculum by grade band is designed; content for the first course (grades 3–4) is in production.

Division · Field Automation

Field Automation — Field-OS Voice Assistant

Turns calls and site notes into draft daily work logs, reducing what slips through in field operations. AI drafts; humans approve.

Problem

Field calls vanish without a record

Instructions and changes travel by phone, daily reports pile up, and what slips through eventually costs money.

Approach

Call → site matching → draft report

Call contents are matched to each site and auto-drafted into the daily work log. Draft information is kept separate from decisions that need human review.

Now

Running internally on real sites

Operating and validating on actual job sites. Public scope will be announced after review.

Healthcare · How it works

How the AI Health Coach works

STEP 1

Connect

Link your Garmin or Galaxy Watch account once — activities, sleep and heart rate flow in automatically.

STEP 2

Two photos

One when you eat, one when you measure body composition. Everything else is automatic.

STEP 3

Cross-read

Weight, muscle, sleep and diet are read together. No scaremongering over a single number.

STEP 4

One line each morning

Delivered to your messenger: exactly what to do today, nothing more.

Data & Privacy

How we handle your data

The same principles apply to every JYLAB product. Health data is treated as sensitive information. No product is a medical device, and none provides diagnosis or treatment advice. Collected data is used only to deliver the features you asked for.

Data we use

Only what you consent to

Only data you explicitly connect or upload. The Health Coach's Garmin integration follows the official Health API user-consent flow.

What we never do

No selling, no ads, no third parties

We do not sell data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties. It is never used beyond the features you requested.

You stay in control

Disconnect or delete anytime

You can disconnect integrations at any time, and we delete your data promptly upon request. Details in our Privacy Policy.

About

About JYLAB

JYLAB is a one-person product studio in South Korea solving everyday problems with AI and automation. We ship a product only after the tool we built for ourselves becomes good enough.

We run four divisions — healthcare, hardware, education and field automation — plus a lab. The AI Health Coach started the same way: one person's automation for runs, body composition and meals is becoming a coach for everyone with the same problem.

Principles
Automated logging, data-driven interpretation, one-line conclusions. Health data is treated as sensitive information with data-minimization and purpose-limitation principles.
