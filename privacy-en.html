2. Purpose of Use

  • Generating AI-based wellness coaching content (daily briefs, weekly reports)
  • Integrated analysis and trends across activity, sleep, body composition and diet
  • Service improvement and error analysis (statistical purposes, pseudonymized/anonymized)

The service provides general wellness information only and is not a medical device or medical service for diagnosis or treatment.

4. Retention Period

  • Data is deleted without delay upon service withdrawal (account disconnection), and completely erased within 30 days at the latest.
  • Where retention is required by law (e.g., 5 years for e-commerce contract/payment records), data is kept only for the legally mandated period.

5. User Rights

  • Users may request access, correction, deletion, or suspension of processing of their data at any time.
  • Wearable account connections can be revoked at any time; new collection stops immediately upon revocation.
  • Contact: jaeyoon1725@gmail.com (handled within 7 business days)

6. Security Measures

  • Sensitive data including health data is encrypted at rest and in transit.
  • Access is restricted to minimum personnel and access logs are retained.
  • External integration tokens are stored encrypted; plaintext passwords are never retained.
